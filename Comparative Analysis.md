# Fraud vs. Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Analysis
## Executive Summary

This analysis compares common fraud schemes with related AML typologies and explains how investigators evaluate each type of activity using a risk-based approach.

---

Financial Crime Typologies

1. Identity Theft

Description

A criminal obtains another person's personal information to open accounts, apply for credit, or conduct unauthorized transactions.

Fraud Investigator Approach

- Verify customer identity.
- Review login history and authentication records.
- Freeze affected accounts.
- Coordinate customer remediation.
- Document unauthorized activity.

AML Investigator Considerations

- Determine whether stolen identities are being used to facilitate money laundering or establish mule accounts.
- Assess whether additional linked accounts require review.

---

2. Money Laundering

Description

The process of disguising the origin of illegally obtained funds through a series of financial transactions.

AML Investigator Approach

- Review customer profile.
- Analyze transaction patterns.
- Evaluate source of funds.
- Conduct Customer Due Diligence (CDD).
- Escalate activity for SAR consideration when appropriate.

---

3. Account Takeover

Description

Unauthorized access to an existing customer account through stolen credentials or compromised authentication.

Fraud Investigator Approach

- Lock the account.
- Verify customer identity.
- Review device and login activity.
- Reverse unauthorized transactions when possible.

AML Investigator Considerations

- Evaluate whether the compromised account is being used to move illicit funds through the financial system.

---

4. Structuring

Description

Breaking large cash transactions into smaller amounts to avoid regulatory reporting thresholds.

AML Investigator Approach

- Review transaction frequency and timing.
- Aggregate related cash deposits.
- Assess customer transaction history.
- Determine whether behavior indicates potential evasion of reporting requirements.
- Document findings for escalation if warranted.

---

5. Credit Card Fraud

Description

Unauthorized use of a payment card to make purchases or obtain funds.

Fraud Investigator Approach

- Review transaction history.
- Block compromised cards.
- Investigate merchant activity.
- Coordinate card replacement and customer reimbursement.

AML Investigator Considerations

- Determine whether fraud proceeds are being transferred through other accounts or used to support broader money laundering activity.

---

6. Shell Companies

Description

A legally registered company with little or no legitimate business operations that may be used to conceal beneficial ownership or move funds.

AML Investigator Approach

- Review ownership structure.
- Verify beneficial ownership information.
- Analyze business purpose and transaction activity.
- Assess consistency between stated operations and financial behavior.
- Recommend Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) when appropriate.

---

7. Phishing

Description

A social engineering attack designed to obtain sensitive customer information through deceptive communications.

Fraud Investigator Approach

- Investigate unauthorized account access.
- Identify compromised credentials.
- Coordinate incident response.
- Notify affected customers.

AML Investigator Considerations

- Evaluate whether compromised accounts are being used to receive or transfer illicit proceeds.

---

8. Terrorist Financing

Description

The collection or movement of funds intended to support terrorist organizations or activities.

AML Investigator Approach

- Conduct sanctions and watchlist screening.
- Review transaction destinations and counterparties.
- Analyze payment patterns.
- Escalate suspicious activity according to institutional policies.
- Support regulatory reporting and law enforcement requests when required.

---

Key Differences

Fraud| AML
Protects customer assets| Protects the financial system
Focuses on unauthorized activity| Focuses on suspicious financial behavior
Customer-centered investigation| Risk-based investigation
Immediate account remediation| Ongoing monitoring and regulatory compliance
Financial loss prevention| Financial crime detection and reporting

Portfolio Takeaway

This analysis demonstrates an understanding of the distinct yet complementary roles of Fraud and AML investigations. While fraud investigators focus on protecting customers from unauthorized activity and financial loss, AML investigators evaluate customer behavior, transaction patterns, and risk indicators to identify potential money laundering or terrorist financing and determine whether escalation or regulatory reporting is warranted. Recognizing these differences is essential for supporting an effective financial crime compliance program.
