# Project Type: Transaction Monitoring & Data Visualization

Reporting Period: April 1–30, 2026


## Executive Summary
This dashboard was developed to simulate the daily monitoring activities of an Anti-Money Laundering (AML) analyst. Using fictional transaction data, the dashboard provides insight into suspicious transaction trends, customer risk exposure, investigator workload, and alert management.

The objective was to demonstrate the ability to transform raw transaction data into actionable intelligence that supports AML investigations, operational decision-making, and regulatory compliance.

Dashboard Visualizations

1. Suspicious Transactions by Day

Visualization: Line Chart

Purpose:
Track daily fluctuations in suspicious transaction activity and identify unusual spikes requiring further investigation.

Key Findings:

- Average of 5 alerts per day
- Peak activity occurred on April 18 (14 alerts)
- Weekend transaction volume was consistently lower than weekdays

Business Value:
Helps investigators identify emerging trends and allocate investigative resources during periods of increased activity.

Analytical Observations

- Structuring alerts represented the largest share of suspicious activity during the reporting period.
- Cross-border wire transfers involving higher-risk jurisdictions generated the highest number of escalations.
- Most investigations were completed within the institution's target service-level agreement.
- Daily alert volumes remained stable, with occasional spikes requiring temporary resource reallocation.

---

Recommendations

- Continue enhanced monitoring of customers conducting frequent international wire transfers.
- Prioritize investigation of structuring alerts due to their frequency.
- Review transaction thresholds quarterly to ensure monitoring scenarios remain effective.
- Rebalance investigator caseloads during periods of elevated alert volume.
- Incorporate dashboard metrics into monthly AML governance reporting.
