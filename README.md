# High-Risk Customer Review (Customer Due Diligence)
## Executive Summary

As part of the bank's periodic Customer Due Diligence (CDD) process, a customer account was reviewed after automated monitoring identified increased international transaction activity. The objective was to evaluate whether the customer's activity remained consistent with the profile established during onboarding and to determine whether the overall customer risk rating should be updated.

After reviewing the customer's occupation, source of funds, geographic exposure, transaction history, Politically Exposed Person (PEP) screening, and sanctions screening results, the customer was assigned a High Risk rating due to the combination of multiple risk factors requiring Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD).

The customer owns a consulting business that facilitates international trade between suppliers and manufacturers in multiple countries.

Rationale

The customer was assigned a High Risk rating based on the cumulative impact of several legitimate but higher-risk characteristics:

- Business operations involve frequent cross-border financial activity.
- Regular international wire transfers increase exposure to financial crime risks.
- Transactions involve jurisdictions requiring enhanced monitoring.
- Higher transaction volume than a typical retail customer.

Although no suspicious activity, sanctions concerns, or adverse screening results were identified during this review, the customer's profile meets the institution's criteria for Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) due to the inherent risks associated with the nature of the business and geographic exposure.

---

Recommendation

- Maintain the customer relationship.
- Upgrade the customer to High Risk status.
- Perform Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD).
- Review supporting business documentation annually.
- Continue ongoing transaction monitoring.
- Rescreen against sanctions and PEP databases as part of routine monitoring.
- Escalate any material changes in transaction behavior for additional investigation.

---

Investigator Closing Notes

This review illustrates the application of a risk-based approach to Customer Due Diligence. While no evidence of suspicious activity was identified, the customer's business model and international exposure justify a higher risk classification and enhanced ongoing monitoring. The recommendation reflects prudent risk management rather than a conclusion of wrongdoing, aligning with AML best practices.
